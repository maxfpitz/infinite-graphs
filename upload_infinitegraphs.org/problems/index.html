Infinite Graphs

Open problems

The book states its 35 numbered problems and 3 named conjectures as of August 2026. This page records progress on them since then. Each is listed with its number in the book, the section where it is discussed and a short description of its topic. The precise statements are in the book.

Recent progress 1 update, newest first

  • 24 November 2026 · Problem 6 (well-balanced orientations) · Solved

    Assem, Mahmoud and Miraftab prove that every multigraph, of any cardinality, has a well-balanced orientation. In particular, every 2k-edge-connected graph has a k-arc-connected orientation, as Nash-Williams had announced in 1960.

    A. Assem, M. A. Mahmoud and B. Miraftab, Nash-Williams' orientation theorems for infinite graphs, arXiv:2611.33366 (2026) (preprint)
